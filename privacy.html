Privacy Policy

SparkCart Poster · Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Overview

SparkCart Poster ("the Service") is a personal, self-hosted tool that the operator uses to schedule and publish their own videos to their own social media accounts. This policy explains what information the Service handles and what it does with it.

2. Information the Service handles

When a social media account is connected, for example through TikTok Login Kit, the Service receives:

  • Basic profile information the platform shares with it, such as the account's display name, username and profile picture.
  • An access token that lets the Service post to that account on the owner's behalf.

The Service also stores the videos, titles, captions and schedule details that the operator enters in order to publish posts.

3. How the information is used

This information is used only to connect the account, to show which account is connected, and to publish the posts that the account owner chooses. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Where the information is stored

The Service runs on the operator's own computer. Account details, tokens and posts are stored in the Service's own database on that computer. They are not stored on a third-party server run by the Service.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. Information is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, to TikTok or YouTube) in order to publish the posts that the account owner has chosen.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is kept only as long as the account stays connected. An account owner can disconnect the Service at any time, and can remove the Service from the list of connected apps in the platform's settings (for TikTok, in the account's security and permissions settings). Removing the connection revokes the Service's access, and the stored token can be deleted from the Service by removing the channel. To ask for any other stored information to be deleted, contact us at the address below.

7. Security

Access to the Service is limited to its operator, and tokens are kept private and are never published. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about stored information, can be sent to sparkcartco@outlook.com.
