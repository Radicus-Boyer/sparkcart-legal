Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how SparkCart ("we") handles information on this website, in our browser games, and in our scheduling tool, SparkCart Poster ("the Service").

Who uses the Service

SparkCart Poster is a private tool used only by SparkCart to publish its own videos to TikTok and YouTube accounts that SparkCart owns. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • TikTok account info: when the account owner signs in with TikTok, we receive basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) so the Service can show which account is posting.
  • Access tokens: TikTok provides tokens that let the Service upload videos for the owner. They are stored on our own server and used only for posting.
  • Content the owner uploads: videos, captions and post settings.

What we do not collect

We collect only what is needed to run the Service. We do not collect data about viewers or other TikTok users, location, contacts, or payment details. We do not run accounts or sign-ups on this website.

How we use it

Only to upload and schedule videos to the connected account and to show posting status. We do not sell, rent or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage, security and retention

Data is kept on a private server controlled by SparkCart. Access tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected. Other stored data is deleted when no longer needed or when you ask.

Visitors to this website and our games

This website does not use cookies, tracking or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. Our games may save progress in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device. See also our cookie information.

Third parties

We use TikTok and YouTube's official tools to post our own content. Those services have their own privacy policies. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics code on this website.

Your choices and data deletion

You can end the Service's access at any time by removing it from the connected apps in your TikTok account settings. To have your data deleted, email contact@sparkcart.fyi with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete it within 30 days. See data deletion.

Children

Our website and the Service are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
