SparkCart Poster

A personal video scheduling tool by SparkCart

SparkCart Poster is a small, self-hosted tool that I use to schedule and publish my own original videos to my own social media accounts, including my TikTok account (@sparkcart.co) and my YouTube channel (SparkCart).

How it works

  • I sign in once to authorize my own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit.
  • I choose a video that I made, write the title and caption, and pick the privacy level and interaction settings myself.
  • I confirm the post, and the tool sends the video to my account using the TikTok Content Posting API.

The tool only posts content that I created, and it does not add watermarks, logos or promotional text to my videos.
