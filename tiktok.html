Permissions we request and why

Product / scopeWhat we use it for
Login Kit: user.info.basicShows the connected account's display name and avatar so the owner can confirm which account is posting.
Content Posting API: video.uploadSends a finished video to the account owner's TikTok inbox as a draft.
Content Posting API: video.publishPosts a finished video directly to the account owner's own profile at the scheduled time.

We request only these permissions. We do not read other users' data, comments, messages or follower lists.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok and approves access.
  2. The owner picks a finished video, writes a caption and chooses privacy, comment, duet and stitch settings.
  3. The video is uploaded at the chosen time, posted directly or sent to the TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. The owner can disconnect the app at any time.

Data we handle

  • From TikTok: the connected account's open ID, display name and avatar, plus the access token needed to upload.
  • From the owner: the videos, captions and post settings they choose.
  • Not collected: data about viewers or other TikTok users, device identifiers, location, contacts or payment details.
  • Use: only to upload and schedule videos and show posting status. Never sold, rented, shared or used for advertising.
  • Storage: on a private server controlled by SparkCart. Access tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected.
  • Third-party code: no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are used on this website or in the app.

Data deletion

You can have your data removed in either of these ways:

  • Disconnect in TikTok: remove SparkCart Poster from the connected apps in your TikTok account settings. This ends the app's access.
  • Ask us: email contact@sparkcart.fyi with the subject "Delete my data". We delete stored tokens, profile info and uploaded content within 30 days and confirm by email.
